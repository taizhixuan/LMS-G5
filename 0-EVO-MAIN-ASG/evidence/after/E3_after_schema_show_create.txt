======================================================================
Known limitation — surface to Stage 2 (E1)
======================================================================
The existing librarian/borrow.php datepicker emits due_date as
DD/MM/YYYY (e.g. '30/06/2026'). librarian/borrow_save.php passes it
straight into the INSERT. Against the new DATE column, MariaDB
silently coerces this to '0000-00-00' instead of rejecting it.

Until E1 rewrites borrow_save.php with proper validation +
DD/MM/YYYY -> YYYY-MM-DD conversion, the UI will record zero-dates
on new borrows. All existing data is unaffected.
