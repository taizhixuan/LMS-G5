Proves: the new foreign key constraint actively blocks deletes that
would orphan borrow rows. Before E3, the borrow table used MyISAM
and had no relational integrity enforcement at the DB level.

======================================================================
Test 1 — Attempting to delete a member with active borrow history
======================================================================
Command:
  mysql -u root --port=3307 jnv -e "DELETE FROM member WHERE member_id = 52;"

Member 52 is referenced by borrow.borrow_id = 482.

Result:
  ERROR 1451 (23000) at line 1: Cannot delete or update a parent row:
  a foreign key constraint fails
  (`jnv`.`borrow`, CONSTRAINT `fk_borrow_member`
   FOREIGN KEY (`member_id`) REFERENCES `member` (`member_id`)
   ON UPDATE CASCADE)

Status: ✅ FK rejected the destructive delete. Before E3, this
DELETE would have succeeded silently and left borrow.member_id = 52
pointing at a non-existent member.
